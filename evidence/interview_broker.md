# Broker / transporter interview (15 minutes, Hindi or Hinglish)

Record: date, city/transport nagar, agency size (people, trucks placed per month), lanes, contract vs spot share.
Ask permission to note answers; don't pitch until the last question.

1. Kal ka din batayiye: subah se shaam tak kitni inquiry aayi, kahan se (WhatsApp, call, email, TMS)?
2. Ek inquiry se shipper ko rate dene mein kitna time lagta hai? Sabse zyada time kis cheez mein jaata hai?
3. Gaadi kaise dhoondhte hain? (apne owners ko call, WhatsApp groups, Vahak/BlackBuck, agent) Kitne log call karte hain ek load ke liye?
4. Pichhle mahine kitne load chhoot gaye kyunki rate ya gaadi time pe nahi mili? Kitne mein gaadi aakhri waqt pe mana kar gayi?
5. Rate kaise tay karte hain? Kitni baar shipper ko diya rate baad mein ghaate wala nikla?
6. Booking ke baad sabse zyada sar-dard kya hai? (gaadi number, bilty, e-way bill, advance, POD, payment)
7. Coordinator chhod ke jaata hai to owners ke number bhi le jaata hai? Kya hua tha pichhli baar?
8. Abhi kaun sa software use karte hain (Tally, bilty software, TMS)? Kitna kharcha?
9. (Demo dikhakar) Is mein kya bekaar hai? Kya cheez ho to kal se use karenge?
10. Agar ye har mahine ₹3,000–5,000 ka ho, ya ₹60–100 har booked load, to kya lenge? 3 hafte free pilot ke liye taiyaar?

After the call: 3 quotes in their own words, the numbers from Q2, Q4, Q5, and a yes/no on the pilot.
