# Truck owner interview (10 minutes)

Record: date, base city, number of trucks and types, lanes, who answers the phone (owner, driver, munshi).

1. Gaadi khaali hone par load kaise dhoondhte ho? Kitne brokers se kaam lete ho?
2. Subah koi WhatsApp pe pooche "gaadi khaali hai?", to jawab doge? Kis time?
3. Rate kaise bolte ho: per km, total, per ton? Mol-bhaav phone pe ya message pe?
4. Advance kitna chahiye aur balance kitne din mein? Detention ka kya hota hai?
5. Kabhi booking ke baad mana kiya? Kyun? Broker ne kya kiya?
6. Bot se baat karna theek lagega agar rate aur payment saaf ho? Voice note bhejna aasaan hai?
7. Hazardous / ODC / reefer maal lete ho? Permit hai?

After the call: reply-time habit, preferred rate format, advance terms, willingness to answer a daily check-in.
